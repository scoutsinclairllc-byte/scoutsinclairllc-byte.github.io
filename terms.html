Hushaby Autopilot: Terms of Service

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Hushaby Autopilot is an internal tool operated by Scout Sinclair LLC. It is used only by Scout Sinclair LLC to manage its own YouTube channels. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and there are no outside users.

Use of YouTube API Services

Hushaby Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. By using Hushaby Autopilot, the user (Scout Sinclair LLC) agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool does

  • Reads the owner's own channel and video statistics.
  • Uploads videos and thumbnails created by Scout Sinclair LLC to the owner's own channels.

The tool does not access, collect or store any other user's YouTube or Google data.

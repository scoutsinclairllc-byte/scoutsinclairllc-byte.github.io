Hushaby Autopilot

Hushaby Autopilot is a private, internal tool run by Scout Sinclair LLC. It prepares and uploads gentle lullabies and baby sleep sounds to the owner's own YouTube channels, Hushaby Moon and Puddle Jump Sing-Along.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
